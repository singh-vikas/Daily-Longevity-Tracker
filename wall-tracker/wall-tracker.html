🌅 MORNING ROUTINE

Priming & Energy

6:00–6:30 AM — Wake & Activate

⏰ Immediate upon waking (<2 min)
  • No phone/screens (preserve dopamine baseline)
  • Get out of bed immediately
  • Morning sunlight 5–10 min, bare eyes (cloudy: 10–20 min)
  • Warm water + lime (500 ml)
  • Meditation / breathing 10–15 min (box breathing or Anulom-Vilom)

6:30–7:30 AM — Exercise

⏰ Log everything you did — combining is encouraged (e.g. strength + short Zone 2). Weekly targets: Resistance ×3 · Zone 2 40–45 min ×3–4 · HIIT ×1–2 + daily post-meal walks.
  • Pre-workout prep (transition, mental prep; L-Tyrosine optional)
  • Strength training (aim ×3/wk — muscle + insulin sensitivity)
  • Zone 2 cardio 40–45 min (aim ×3–4/wk — raises HDL, lowers TG)
  • HIIT / Zone 5 intervals (aim ×1–2/wk — best lever for low HDL 32)
  • Light/recovery mobility (Surya Namaskar / stretching)
  • Post-workout cool-down (wait 10–15 min before shower)

7:30–8:00 AM — Shower, Skin Care & Green Tea

⏰ 90–120 min after waking. Cold exposure NOT right after strength training.
  • Shower (cold exposure optional, 2–3×/wk)
  • Skin care: face wash → Vitamin C serum → moisturizer → sunscreen SPF 30+ (non-negotiable)
  • Green tea (fine in AM; keep ≥2 h away from the 3:30 PM iron dose)

8:00–8:45 AM — Deep Work

  • Deep work session (45 min — hardest tasks first)
  • Phone in another room

🍳 Breakfast (8:45–9:15 AM, or 10:00 AM if 15-h fast)

⏰ Per diet plan: last bite ~7 PM → first meal 10 AM (15-h fast — best for TG 227).
  • High-protein meal (30–40 g protein) — +1 scoop whey/pea isolate (daily, for 135 g/day target)
  • No red meat · salmon Tue/Thu · low-fat paneer only
  • Post-breakfast walk (15 min)

🌿 Morning Supplements (with breakfast)

  • Vitamin D3 — 50,000 IU ONCE WEEKLY (Sun) 🩺 with fattiest meal (Vit D was 10 ng/mL)
  • Omega-3 ~2 g EPA/DHA 🩺 (lowers TG, raises HDL)
  • Creatine 5 g (muscle + strength; any consistent time)
  • Optional — CoQ10 · Vitamin K2 · Turmeric (away from iron) · NR/NMN · Spermidine
  • Vitamin C moved to the 3:30 PM iron slot · Vitamin E dropped

☀️ AFTERNOON ROUTINE

Performance & Maintenance

🍽 Lunch (12:00–1:00 PM)

⏰ Psyllium 1 tsp + water 10–15 min before the meal.
  • Apple cider vinegar + psyllium husk (before meal)
  • High-protein meal (25–35 g) + steamed veg + fermented foods
  • No red meat · foxtail millet not brown rice · 1 tsp EVOO/mustard oil
  • Post-lunch walk (10–15 min)

🌿 Lunch Supplements — ⚪ all optional

  • Vitamin K2 · Garlic extract · B-Complex (Mon & Thu)
  • Magnesium moved to evening (keeps it ≥5 h from the iron dose)

1:00–3:30 PM — Work & Energy

  • 1:00–2:00 Light cognitive tasks (email, admin, meetings)
  • 2:00–2:15 Green tea (finish ≥90 min before the 3:30 iron) + break
  • 2:15–3:30 Collaborative work / study

3:30–4:00 PM — NSDR + Snack

⏰ NSDR before 4 PM consolidates learning & addresses the 2–3 PM dip.
  • NSDR / Yoga Nidra 10–20 min
  • Snack — dairy-free, with a Vitamin C source (orange/berries)

🌿 3:30 PM — Iron Anchor (essential, every day)

⚠️ Dairy-free · no tea/coffee within 2 h · not with turmeric/calcium.
  • Iron — ferrous bisglycinate 25–50 mg 🩺 (ferritin 14 ng/mL)
  • Vitamin C 500 mg (doubles iron absorption)
  • Ashwagandha 600 mg — optional (snack OR evening, not both)

4:00–6:00 PM — Wrap-Up & Movement

  • Plan next day (3–4 key tasks) + journal reflection
  • Light stretching / walk / evening workout if morning missed
  • Hydration 500–750 ml

🌙 EVENING ROUTINE

Recovery & Reflection

  • Light review / reading · plan tomorrow's meals · hydration

🍽 Dinner (7:00–8:00 PM)

⏰ 3–4 h before bed. Lighter than lunch. Psyllium before.

8:15–9:00 PM — Skin Care & Wind-Down

  • Skin care: cleanse → retinol/niacinamide (alternate) → moisturizer → eye cream

  • Journal & gratitude (3 things)

💊 Evening Supplements & Medication (9–10 PM)

9:00–9:30 PM — Sleep Prep

⏰ Target bed 9:30–10:00 PM · 7–9 h · consistent wake ±30 min.
  • Cool (18–20 °C), dark, quiet · phone out of room
